## Biography

I was born in Italy in 1995, moved to Germany in 2019 and to Switzerland in 2024.

I am interested in chemistry, its role in our society and how to use it to solve problems.

In my free time, I like to play music, hike, and think about abstract concepts.

## Curriculum

2019–2023 — Max Planck Institute of Colloids and Interfaces (PhD, Materials Science)

2014–2019 — Università degli Studi di Trieste (Bachelor's and Master's degrees, Chemistry)
