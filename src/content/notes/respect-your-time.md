I am an avid user of Youtube, which has basically become my source of information like a personalized magazine. Among the content I consume, one format that has attracted my attention is the [*... but I respect your time*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=foO1QakhjZE&pp=ygUXYnV0IGkgcmVzcGVjdCB5b3VyIHRpbWU%3D) where a review of a product is provided in 2-3 minutes instead of the traditional 20-30 minutes video essay. *This meeting could have been an email* feels the same way, expressing the frustration when information are not delivered efficiently. I experience the same feeling when interacting with a Large Language Model, trying to understand hard concepts from a scattered literature, becoming worse and worse when the same slop is published freely on LinkedIn. 

This problem has become worse with the advent of LLM and it has highlighted how much attention is the real currency when content production is not the limiting step.
