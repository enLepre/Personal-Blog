I am an avid user of Youtube, which has basically become my source of information like a personalized magazine. Among the content I consume, one format that has attracted my attention is the [*... but I respect your time*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=foO1QakhjZE&pp=ygUXYnV0IGkgcmVzcGVjdCB5b3VyIHRpbWU%3D) where a review of a product is provided in 2-3 minutes instead of the traditional 20-30 minutes video essay. *This meeting could have been an email* feels the same way, expressing the frustration when information are not delivered efficiently. I experience the same feeling when interacting with a Large Language Model, trying to understand hard concepts from a scattered literature, becoming worse and worse when the same slop is published freely on LinkedIn. I open Substack trying to find higher quality information, but I end up scrolling through endless essays saying over and over the same thing. I read this same text, finding it difficult to not feel the same frustration and I ask myself: *what is the point of all of this?* 

Writing has never been my sharpest skill. I really got into it only during my PhD because writing a scientific argument felt like water flowing in the mountains. You know where you are starting and where you should finish, and you are building the river bed opening your chain of thoughts to your audience. The more I got conscious of this process, the more I have started to be a more critical reader. The hard constrains of the scientific text does not allow for rethoric figures and it forces you to think deeply on every sentence I construct: *Is it true? Who is saying the opposite? Why are they saying the opposite?* I have started to ask the same questions on the texts I read as well, helping me to understand more about the reader rather than the topic. The same ineffiencies I complained before allows for the personal connection, even if the personal connection is anger. *Where is the limit then?*

Length matters. When I scroll the an essay and the lateral bar is the size of my thumb I get discouraged. I entered that page with the intention of learning about hydrogen economy, why am I reading about roman empire?
