I am an avid user of Youtube, which has basically become my source of information like a personalized magazine. Among the content I consume, one format that has attracted my attention is the [*... but I respect your time*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=foO1QakhjZE&pp=ygUXYnV0IGkgcmVzcGVjdCB5b3VyIHRpbWU%3D) where a review of a product which is often delivered in 20-30 minutes is provided in 2-3 minutes instead. on the same line, *this meeting could have been an email* feels the same way, expressing the frustration when information are not delivered efficiently. AI slop is not different as well, and when interacting with a Large Language Model it often feels like an endless [and mill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ant_mill).
