I am an avid user of Youtube, which has basically become my source of information like a personalized magazine. Among the content I consume, one format that has attracted my attention is the [*... but I respect your time*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=foO1QakhjZE&pp=ygUXYnV0IGkgcmVzcGVjdCB5b3VyIHRpbWU%3D) where a review of a product which is often delivered in 20-30 minutes is provided in 2-3 minutes instead. The same concept is held in the joke *this 30 minutes meeting could have been a two sentence email". Both examples highlights the frustratration in engaing with information based on their content and not their shape.
