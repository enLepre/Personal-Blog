Organizations of people serve as a tool to focus individual efforts towards problems too large to be addressed by single persons. However, organization are not always efficient as they need guiding principles  

> when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure.

That's [Goodhart's law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodhart%27s_law) describing how we fail to evaluate performance of individuals or organization when we rely on metrics. It is a hurtfull concept when you think quantification is the key to solving problem. Yet, looking into the development of academic metrics it becomes very clear how it works. _Publish or perish_ is the exact consenquence when we try to evaluate the quality of researchers
