> when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure.

That's [Goodhart's law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodhart%27s_law) describing how we fail to evaluate performance of individuals or organization when we rely on metrics. It is a hurtfull concept when you think quantification is the key to solving problem.
