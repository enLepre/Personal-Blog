It is very difficult to judge the quality of work when it comes to research in science. While there are certain red flags you can be mindful consider, the same red flags might be changing if you consider the background of the researcher. for a physicists preparing a solution might be a big challenge whereas for a chemist is not. Hence, a chemist seeing a physicist preparing a solution might be fast in concluding bad quality, but wrong in not considering the background of the experiment.

Judgement is also a Damocle's sword for everyone to gain advantage over other peers in a more and more fierce competition for a position. It is only natural that human beings following incentives would use criticism on other collegues to take advantage of their welanesses. However, they will loose the point of what research means.

Research means investiganting unknown avanues where unexpected consequences will arise. More often than expected, those consequences appear obvious only after the damage is done. Yet, the same damage looks like impossible before. For this reason, experimenting and making mistakes is fundamental to uncover deeper truths about our universe. 

Using judgment on other mistakes as a weapon to define hirrarchy goes against the point of this process. It makes researcher less willing to take risks and increase their fear in designing new experiments. I think this is a battle to fight in a group to allow everyone from bahelors to profesors the space for imperfections. The same space can provide everyone new important information which would otherwise be lost. 

So, next time you see an obvious mistake, dont judge. Think about the background, ask if help is needed, and be ready in support was the mistake will be uncovered. Not for judging, but for growing together because, at the end, if you are not making mistakes, you are not working in hard enough problems.
