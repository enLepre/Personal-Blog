Carbon materials are ubiquitous playing a role in everyday tasks such as writing with graphite or special moments such as a marriage proposal with a diamond. Carbons are central not only for daily life experience, but they find application in modern day engineering, giving options to clean water from pollutants, transform raw chemical materials in more valuable ones, or store charges in batteries or capacitors. 

Despite their long life and presence on the technological market, still many things are there to be uncovered. Modern-day carbon materials are part of the nanotechnology world including wonder materials such as graphene or carbon nanotubes, whose synthesis is expensive and limits their breathtaking properties to seeing the light of the market. Yet, not only graphene should take the spotlight as doped carbons and porous carbons are underdogs heroes which allow for many technologies to be developed at a cost-effective realization. 

Part of my research was based on such materials. I have shown how to obtain [highly oxidative resistance materials](https://pubs.acs.org/aamick/article/13/25/29612/369311/Caffeine-Derived-Noble-Carbons-as-Ball-Milling) easing the processing for electrode materials in capacitors. I have shown how such oxidation resistance can be used to generate [new catalyst with unique properties](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2211285522002725?via%3Dihub) for electrochemical conversions. Finally, I have shown how much the content of the heteroatoms can change the properties when tuned in [unprecedented loadings](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/anie.202211663).
